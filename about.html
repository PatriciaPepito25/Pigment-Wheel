ABOUT US

Pigment Wheel

Pigment Wheel is a creative studio dedicated to exploring the vibrant intersection of color, art, and technology. We believe that color is more than decoration. It is a language that communicates emotion, culture, and identity. Our team combines artistic intuition with technical precision to craft experiences that are both visually striking and deeply meaningful.

Vision

1

To make color design accessible and inspiring for creators of all backgrounds.

2

To bridge the gap between traditional art techniques and modern digital tools.

3

To build a global community that celebrates bold, expressive, and joyful visual storytelling.
